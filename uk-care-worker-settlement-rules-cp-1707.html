For UK Sponsor Licence holders, the reforms create a sharp divide around the 12 May 2025 cut-off, a £50,271 salary cliff-edge, and a new 180-day displaced care worker window.

Direct Answer: What is Shabana Mahmood’s New Settlement Rule for Care Workers?

Executive Summary for Sponsors

Under Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood’s “Earned Settlement” reforms (set out in Command Paper CP 1707 following a consultation where 84% of respondents opposed the initial proposals), the standard qualifying period for Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) doubles from 5 years to 10 years. However, the Home Secretary made four critical concessions affecting care workers and health/care sponsors:

1. 15-Year Wait Dropped for Sub-RQF6 Roles

The consultation proposal to force workers in roles below degree level (under RQF Level 6, including care workers, senior care workers, nursing auxiliaries, and hospitality/logistics staff) onto a punitive 15-year route has been scrapped. The maximum baseline for compliant workers is 10 years.

2. Pre-12 May 2025 Care Workers Keep 5-Year ILR

Care workers and senior care workers who entered or switched into the route before 12 May 2025 (the publication date of the Immigration White Paper) retain their 5-year route to settlement, provided they earn at least £25,000/year, fulfill genuine sponsorship requirements, and pass the new mandatory baseline.

3. "Second Chance" 5-Year Care Visa for Exploited Staff

Pre-May 2025 care workers who do not meet the £25,000 salary or work requirements at Year 5 (affecting an estimated 50,000 workers due to rogue sponsors or under-hours) will not be refused outright—they can apply for a further 5-year Health and Care visa (with NRPF) restricted to genuine care work.

4. 180-Day Displaced Care Worker Window (1 Feb 2027)

From 1 February 2027, care workers whose employer loses their sponsor licence get 120 days to secure a new sponsored job offer plus 60 further days (180 days total) to resolve their visa status—creating a major domestic recruitment pool for compliant sponsors.

Legal Implementation Status: There is no start date yet for the overarching 10-year rules (though B2 English starts 26 March 2027 and the 180-day care rule starts 1 February 2027). Until the Home Office lays the Statement of Changes, current 5-year rules apply on a decision-date basis.

Technical Policy Trap Identified in CP 1707

The £142 Care Worker Dependant Income Discrepancy

Chapter 8 states that pre-12 May 2025 care workers qualify for 5-year ILR on a salary of £25,000. However, Chapter 2 states that where a couple combines income to cover a non-working or low-earning dependant partner, the required household income threshold is £25,142 (twice the £12,571 individual tax threshold).

Business Recommendation: If you employ a pre-May 2025 care worker on exactly £25,000 who is the sole earner for a dependant partner, offering just 12–15 hours of paid overtime per year to lift their P60 taxable pay to £25,142+ prevents their partner from being blocked from settling alongside them.
Effective 1 February 2027 • Fair Work Agency (FWA)

The 120 + 60 Day Displaced Worker Rule & CQC/FWA Audits

From 1 February 2027, care workers who lose their employment because their sponsor's licence is revoked get up to 120 days to secure a new job offer from a licensed sponsor, plus a further 60 days (180 days total) to resolve their visa and employment status before curtailment.

Dual Impact for Sponsors: First, compliant care groups can recruit vetted, UK-experienced displaced carers for a full 6 months. Second, the newly established Fair Work Agency (FWA) is partnering with the CQC and DHSC to aggressively revoke licences of sponsors failing to provide contracted hours.

Three Structural Changes That Disrupt Graduate Recruitment & Employee Families

1. Long Residence Abolished

The 15-Year Graduate Hire Trap

The 10-year Long Residence ILR route is abolished. Time spent on Student, Graduate, Youth Mobility, High Potential Individual (HPI), Global Business Mobility, Seasonal Worker, or Ukraine schemes no longer counts towards settlement. A graduate who studied for 4 years, spent 1.5–2 years on a Graduate visa, and switches to a £42,000 Skilled Worker role starts their 10-year clock at zero—facing 15–16 years in the UK before ILR.

2. Chapter 5 Dependant Rule

The Age-25 Child Dependant Cut-Off

Young adults aged 18 to 24 who entered as child dependants can still settle with their parents without meeting the £12,571 earnings floor. However, once a child turns 25 before their parents qualify for ILR, they are cut off from family settlement and must qualify independently. Shifting a parent from a 5-year to a 10-year route will push teenage children past age 25 before the family can settle.

3. Suitability Overhaul

Cautions, Court Fines & B2 English

Previously, only custodial sentences of 12+ months triggered automatic ILR refusal. Under CP 1707, any adult UK criminal record—including police conditional cautions, discharges, and court-imposed fines—bars settlement. In addition, from 26 March 2027, all settlement applicants (except UKRS refugees at A2) must pass CEFR Level B2 English (A-level standard, up from B1).

Frequently Asked Questions

Employer & Sponsor Licence FAQs on Earned Settlement (CP 1707)

When do Shabana Mahmood's new 10-year Earned Settlement rules officially start?

Command Paper CP 1707 (published November 2026) sets out the government's final confirmed policy, but there is no single start date yet for the overarching 10-year qualifying period. The Home Office will publish detailed operational guidance and lay a formal Statement of Changes to the Immigration Rules before the changes take effect. Until that Statement of Changes comes into force, the current 5-year rules continue to apply. However, specific dates are already fixed: the 120+60 day displaced care worker rule starts on 1 February 2027, and the mandatory B2 English language requirement for settlement starts on 26 March 2027.

Will care workers and sub-RQF6 workers face a 15-year wait for ILR?

No. In the biggest shift from the November 2025 consultation proposals, the Home Secretary dropped the proposed 15-year wait for workers in occupations below RQF Level 6. Sub-RQF6 workers who play by the rules will qualify through the standard 10-year pathway (or 5 years if they qualify under the pre-12 May 2025 transitional arrangements for care workers and national pay scale nursing auxiliaries/teaching assistants, or if they earn £50,271+).

How does the "best years" earnings assessment work for staff who take maternity or sick leave?

To prevent temporary periods of maternity, parental, adoption, or sick leave from pushing an employee onto a longer settlement route, the Home Office will assess gross earnings using a "best years" look-back window:
• 3-Year Route (£125,141+): Must meet the threshold in 3 of the 4 years immediately before applying (allowing 1 lower year).
• 5-Year Route (£50,271+): Must meet the threshold in 3 of the 5 years immediately before applying (allowing 2 lower years).
• 10-Year & 20-Year Routes (£12,571+ individual / £25,142 couple): Must meet the threshold in 5 of the 7 years immediately before applying (allowing 2 lower years).

Which healthcare and education roles qualify for the permanent 5-year "Public Service Discount" (Annex C)?

Annex C of CP 1707 lists 31 RQF Level 6+ health and education occupation codes paid according to national pay scales (Table 3 of Appendix Skilled Occupations). These include generalist and specialist medical practitioners (doctors), registered nurses and midwives, pharmacists, optometrists, dental practitioners, medical radiographers, paramedics, podiatrists, physiotherapists, occupational therapists, speech and language therapists, clinical psychologists, social workers, head teachers, and primary, secondary, nursery, SEN, and further education teaching professionals. Workers in these roles keep a permanent 5-year path to ILR even if their salary is below £50,271.

Can refugees employed by our business qualify for settlement faster through work?

Refugees who arrive via designated Safe and Legal routes (such as the UK Resettlement Scheme) qualify for ILR after 5 years with an exemption from the public funds rule and an A2 English requirement. However, adults who claim asylum in the UK outside a safe and legal route are placed on a 20-year "Core Protection" route (or 30 years if they arrived illegally, e.g., via small boat, unless Article 31 applies). By moving into sustained employment (earning £12,571+ for 3 years) or study, they can switch onto the Protection Work and Study route and reduce their pathway by 10 years (to 10 years on that route, or 20 years if they entered illegally).
