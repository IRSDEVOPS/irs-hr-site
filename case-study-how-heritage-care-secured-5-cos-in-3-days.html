The Challenge

Trapped in the "Zero Allocation" Cycle

The Unforgiving Rule

Did you know? If a care provider does not assign any Certificates of Sponsorship (CoS) in the previous calendar year, the Home Office heavily restricts them, often giving them 0 CoS allocation.

This was the exact predicament Heriitage Care faced. They were a growing care provider desperately needing staff to service existing care packages and secure new ones.

  • Zero Allocation: 0 allocation granted in the past 2 years.

  • Unreliable Local Workforce: Local hires frequently left employment shortly after starting, jeopardizing care continuity.

  • Blind Spots: Like many, they used care management software and spreadsheets but lacked a comprehensive view of the sponsorship system, missing critical compliance gaps.

"They wanted to hire sponsored workers and promote existing sponsored care workers to senior carers, but the system was working against them."

Spreadsheets aren't enough.
