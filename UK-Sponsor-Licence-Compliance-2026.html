🚨 Urgent Q4 2026 Updates

The immigration landscape has shifted rapidly. Here is what you must actively manage today.

The Fully Digital Reality

Physical BRPs are gone. You must use the Home Office portal to check share codes and track digital eVisa expiry dates automatically.

HMRC Data Sharing

The Home Office and HMRC now share data automatically. Discrepancies between CoS stated salaries and PAYE reports trigger instant red flags.

Worker Rights Duty

New rules require sponsors to prove they actively educate sponsored workers on UK employment rights (NMW, trade unions, grievances).

SMS Multi-Factor Auth

MFA is now mandatory for all Sponsor Management System users. Ensure Key Personnel logins are secure and handed over properly.
