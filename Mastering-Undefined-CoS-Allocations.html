The CoS Allocation Nightmare

Requesting an increase in undefined CoS is no longer a simple administrative task. It is a high-risk legal and logistical minefield that can paralyze your workforce.

18-Week Wait Times

Standard processing times have exploded. If a current employee's visa is expiring, waiting 18 weeks forces you to choose between dismissing them or risking a £60,000 illegal working penalty while waiting.

The Priority Slot Lottery

Trying to bypass the wait? The Home Office releases just 120 priority slots (£350 fee) at 7 AM daily for tens of thousands of applicants. Slots vanish in seconds, leaving manual HR teams stranded.

Onerous 5-Day Deadlines

Even with priority, the Home Office will often halt processing to demand 6 months of bank statements, org charts, and employment contracts—giving you just 5 working days to compile and submit them.

Industry Focus

The Care Sector is in the Home Office's Crosshairs.

Chronic shortages, high turnover, and reliance on migrant workers make the care sector highly vulnerable. Sponsor licence revocations are at an all-time high, and Home Office scrutiny is relentless.

The Hartford Care Group Precedent

The Home Office recently demanded contracts guaranteeing hours before granting a CoS. In the High Court case of Hartford Care Group, this was deemed irrational. Our panel of solicitors uses this exact legal precedent to fight back against unfair Home Office rejections for our clients.
